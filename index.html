Production concept

One service journey, three human perspectives.

The end-to-end MyVisit experience is the backbone: finding the service, appointment preparation, technician arrival, accurate collection process and confident completion. Three adjacent customer situations add relevance without requiring three separate campaign shoots.

Service-first capture

Protect controlled, operationally correct MyVisit demonstrations, technician interactions, warm neutral b-roll, clean plates, horizontal and intentionally vertical-safe framing, photography and a well-labeled handoff library.

Customer-story design

Proposed situations: a busy adult, an older adult receiving care in a familiar setting, and a caregiver helping a family member. These are scripts inspired by approved experiences, not unverified patient testimonials. LifeLabs must approve portrayals and disclosures.

Non-union casting and permitted use

Three principal performers. Defined 2027 media rights.

Talent plan

  • Non-union talent only; verify eligibility before engagement.
  • Three identifiable principals: busy adult, older adult and caregiver, plus up to two supporting/filler appearances as needed within the existing allowance.
  • Use approved MyVisit technicians where practical; actors portraying service actions require trained/approved portrayal.
  • Role-specific written offers cover session days, screen time, approved footage/stills, versions and paid promotion. Supporting performers must be reclassified if their screen use warrants it.

2027 Canadian digital + CTV/OTT

  • Proposed territory: Canada nationwide, or specifically agreed Canadian regions. Proposed term: January 1–December 31, 2027; actual use after production, approval and signed agreements.
  • Digital includes website, online video, organic and paid social; expanded rights target connected TV and over-the-top streaming advertising.
  • Linear broadcast TV is excluded unless obtainable without meaningful incremental cost and expressly agreed in writing.
  • Confirm likeness/voice, approved stills, cutdowns, reframing, exclusivity if any, and renewal terms in each agreement. No usage is granted before signed acceptance.
