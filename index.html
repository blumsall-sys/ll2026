Production concept

One service journey, three human perspectives.

The end-to-end MyVisit experience is the backbone: finding the service, appointment preparation, technician arrival, accurate collection process and confident completion. Three adjacent customer situations add relevance without requiring three separate campaign shoots.

Service-first capture

Protect controlled, operationally correct MyVisit demonstrations, technician interactions, warm neutral b-roll, clean plates, horizontal and intentionally vertical-safe framing, photography and a well-labeled handoff library.

Customer-story design

Proposed situations: a busy adult, an older adult receiving care in a familiar setting, and a caregiver helping a family member. These are scripts inspired by approved experiences, not unverified patient testimonials. LifeLabs must approve portrayals and disclosures.

Three production decisions

Choose the coverage and deliverables that matter most.

Every option retains BCM’s advanced multi-camera/multi-operator crew, its included camera, lighting and audio equipment, and two coordinated filming days. Expanded adds $1,200 of flexible BCM production enhancement capacity, targeted at an approved staffed alternate angle, supplementary lighting, a second setup, or focused post support. The historical $39,800 Expanded allocation was underfunded for three cast scenarios; the revised cast-adjusted Expanded budget is $43,160 (approximately $43K), before GST. Talent-rights selection stays adjustable through written, separately budgeted agreements—not unpriced promises.

Why Story-First at $36,000 is a meaningful upgrade from Essential at $33,000.

The $3,000 difference increases the funded pool for three principals plus supporting talent, provides more story-focused production and editorial capacity, and doubles the catalog selects runtime from approximately 1 to 2 minutes. The booking walkthrough is deferred in Story-First to concentrate paid hours on three approved customer edits; it is an optional $1,400 / 20-hour add-back. Expanded adds more flexible scene coverage, a 3–5-minute catalog in two purpose-assembled formats, the booking walkthrough, and more finishing flexibility.

Flexible production and rights, with a fixed approved scope.

Before shooting, BCM can propose the most productive use of the Expanded enhancement allocation, and LifeLabs may elect a digital-only or broader-media performer rights schedule. Moving funds between agreed lines requires a written allocation and may change the mix of deliverables; signed performer licensing, new media rights, extra edit time and third-party costs are never assumed to be included for free.

Casting and permitted use

Build performer offers around the intended media use.

Cast to the story—and compensate for screen time

  • Three identifiable customer principals: busy adult, older adult and caregiver; plus up to two supporting/filler appearances.
  • Offer higher session and usage fees to the performer with more dialogue, screen time or required working hours; extras have smaller, expressly defined roles.
  • Use real approved MyVisit technicians where practical; any actors performing service actions need trained/approved portrayal.
  • Make a written, role-specific offer covering shoot day(s), appearance in approved edits/stills, media, term, territory, edit versions and permitted paid promotion.
  • The existing $5,110 Story-First and cast-adjusted Expanded talent allowance is a digital-use planning pool pending successful negotiations.

Optional online + CTV + broadcast talent agreement

  • We can offer each selected non-union eligible performer an all-in 2027 media fee covering Canadian digital/paid social, website, online video, eligible CTV/OTT and linear broadcast use, if accepted and legally permitted.
  • Describe 2027 term and Canada territory, approved footage/stills, cutdowns, reframing, character portrayal, voice/likeness, any category exclusivity and renewal pricing in the agreement.
  • If a performer is union-covered, the applicable ACTRA/UBCP commercial terms and required residuals take priority; a privately drafted flat buyout does not override those obligations.
  • BCM can meet with LifeLabs/media partners to determine anticipated platform, codec, loudness, captioning, duration and clearance requirements before picture lock.
  • The Expanded finishing allocation provides more room after picture lock to prepare an agreed spec-compliant output. New cuts, multiple broadcaster packages or unusual mastering remain separately scoped.

Expanded allocation / practical deployment

Deploy added production capacity where it improves the final library.

Multiple cameras and operators are already part of BCM’s advanced production crew. Expanded preserves those baseline capabilities and adds a $1,200 flexible BCM enhancement allocation. We will recommend its use against the approved two-day shot plan so the added spend produces stronger, more varied, or more efficient coverage—not necessarily another camera running for both days.

Examples of useful deployment

  • Staff a second camera position for a demanding service interaction or one additional customer-scenario scene.
  • Supplement lighting, grip or operator capacity to capture a separate setup without slowing the core service sequence.
  • Plan alternate wides, details and reactions that support the three customer stories and the evergreen selects sequences.
  • Allocate remaining enhancement capacity to an approved finishing or modular coverage requirement where appropriate, with the same budget held constant.

How we control the allocation

The $1,200 is a flexible planning amount, not a promise of all examples. Before shooting, BCM will show the chosen crew/gear/scene/post combination and reconcile it against the same service rates. The normal advanced crew, equipment, photography and agreed edit hours are not reduced without an approved scope change. This protects the main service footage and existing deliverables.
